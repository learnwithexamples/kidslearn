Nothing happening?

  • Press a button first. Chrome hides controllers until you do.
  • Check the cable. Many USB cables only carry power, not data. Try another one.
  • Older Xbox One pad? If the plastic around the Xbox button is part of the top strip with the bumpers, it has no Bluetooth — it needs a cable.
  • Turn your Xbox off. A console nearby will grab the controller back.
  • Try Chrome. Its controller support is the most reliable.
